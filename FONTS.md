# Fonty

Fonty hodin v `ioshell/app/src/main/res/font/clock_*.ttf` pochází z [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts)
a jsou pod licencí **SIL Open Font License 1.1**: Inter, Outfit, Manrope, Oswald, Bebas Neue, Playfair Display,
Big Shoulders Stencil Display, JetBrains Mono. `fonts/Nunito.ttf` (motiv Graphite) je taktéž OFL.
